## Notes

Roadmap S-12: the user enters the quantity of a medicine as they see it — in tablets (pieces) — also for packs the registry describes with a unit count ("1 butelka po 90 tabl.", "2 blistry po 10 tabl."), and the app shows that quantity unambiguously, without mixing "bottles" with "tablets". Touches the kit add form, CSV import, kit list and edit panel.
